## Agree on the roles

You choose the goal, attempt the work, and declare uncertainty. The tutor helps sequence tasks, adjust challenge, and give feedback. Your course sources determine its conventions. An AI should not invent citations, remember sessions it was not given, or call hinted work independent. This website explains the workflow; it does not connect to an AI tutor or collect your answers.

## A portable starting prompt

```text
Topic: [topic]
Goal: [what I want to do independently]
Level and time: [level; time available]
Sources and known gaps: [sources; gaps or unknown]

Begin with a short unassisted diagnosis. Ask one meaningful question at a time. Let me attempt before showing a solution, unless a prerequisite needs teaching. Use the smallest useful hint and record the highest H0–H5 level. Check claims against my sources, name uncertainty, and finish with independent evidence, an open gap, and a delayed-review task.
```

## Ask for one useful action

Use MAP for a partial relationship map, PROOF for checking your argument, HINT 1 for a strategic question, TRANSFER for a new unlabeled problem, RECALL for a closed-notes test, and WRAP for a session handoff. These are conventions for your external tutor conversation, not buttons or services on this page. Before reusing an AI judgment, check the disputed definition or step against the source.
