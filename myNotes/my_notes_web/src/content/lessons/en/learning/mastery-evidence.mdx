## Keep six dimensions separate

<LearningFigure kind="evidence" />

For each dimension, keep a task and its observed result. Can you state a definition precisely, explain its meaning, justify a result, test an assumption, choose a method in a new situation, and do this after a delay? These questions reveal different strengths; a smooth explanation does not settle all of them.

## Use the scale as a record, not a verdict

| Level | What the observation shows |
|---|---|
| NA | Not assessed |
| 0 | Not yet demonstrated |
| 1 | Success with substantial guidance |
| 2 | Partly independent; routine cases with remaining gaps |
| 3 | Accurate and justified without help on representative tasks |
| 4 | Flexible performance on unfamiliar variations and boundaries |

The six dimensions and H0–H5 hint levels measure different things. Record both when helpful. Do not average the profile into a “mastery percentage”. Immediate independent performance is provisional; a different independent task after a delay gives stronger evidence. Confidence can be recorded before trying, then compared with the result.
