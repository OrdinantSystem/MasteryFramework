## The attempt comes before the answer

A familiar answer is easy to recognize. That does not tell you whether you could produce it tomorrow. Close the notes, attempt the definition or problem, then compare. Reading after a failed attempt is useful correction; reading instead of attempting does not give the same evidence.

## Make a few cards worth answering

Use cards for an exact definition, a theorem’s assumptions, a small counterexample, or one short application. Ask one clear question with a checkable answer. For example: “Give a bounded real sequence that does not converge.” The answer (−1)ⁿ still needs a brief justification. Keep full proofs and multi-step problems in problem-solving practice.

Practice testing and distributed practice received high utility ratings in [Dunlosky et al.’s 2013 review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26173288/). That supports the component practices; it does not validate our exact timetable or the entire assembled workflow. Shorten an interval after difficulty, extend it after reliable independent success, and keep the workload manageable.
