## Three questions shape the workflow

The Spanish transcript presents an anecdote about an MIT student mastering a subject in 48 hours. It then demonstrates three prompts using a personal-finance source collection: identify shared mental models, examine expert disagreements, and ask questions that distinguish understanding from repetition. ## Adapt the questions to university work

1. Which definitions and relationships support this topic? Require actual source locations; do not assume all experts share exactly five models.
2. Where do assumptions, methods, or interpretations differ? In mathematics, compare valid proof approaches, boundary conditions, and false converses rather than inventing expert disagreement.
3. What new problem would expose a misunderstanding? Attempt it before reading a solution, then ask for the first specific gap.
