## A useful takeaway, with a boundary

Consider Cornell notes. A question column can become a place for prompts you answer with the notes covered. It can also remain a list you never use. Likewise, copying headings into a mind map does not require you to justify a single connection. Ask what you actually did with the material and what you can do afterwards.

The speaker goes further, claiming that correctly executed mapping “always” works and that flashcards are nearly useless for professionals. Those broad claims are not established here. Reading can supply missing knowledge, and cards can support exact recall; neither needs to perform every learning task to be useful. Our proposed workflow keeps retrieval, mapping, feedback, and application without adopting the grades as evidence or treating mental effort alone as proof of learning.
