## Three small records

<LearningFigure kind="handoff" />

A **session note** records the goal, sources, exact attempts, hints, results, and next review. A **concept card** holds a precise statement, intuition with limits, an example and non-example, proof evidence, and a transfer question. An **error/review log** tracks the first invalid step, repair, new independent attempt, and review task. Keep links to your own work so the result can be checked.
