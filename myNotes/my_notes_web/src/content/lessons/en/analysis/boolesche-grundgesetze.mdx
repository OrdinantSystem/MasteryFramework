## Five starting laws of Boolean algebra

<span id="from-boolesche-grundgesetze-laws-15"></span>

### Laws 1–5

<Math tex="\tag{1}(\colA{A}\lor \colB{B})\lor \colC{C}=\colA{A}\lor(\colB{B}\lor \colC{C}),\quad(\colA{A}\land \colB{B})\land \colC{C}=\colA{A}\land(\colB{B}\land \colC{C})" display />

<Math tex="\tag{2}\colA{A}\lor \colB{B}=\colB{B}\lor \colA{A},\quad \colA{A}\land \colB{B}=\colB{B}\land \colA{A}" display />

<Math tex="\tag{3}\colA{A}\land(\colB{B}\lor \colC{C})=(\colA{A}\land \colB{B})\lor(\colA{A}\land \colC{C})" display />

<Math tex="\colA{A}\lor(\colB{B}\land \colC{C})=(\colA{A}\lor \colB{B})\land(\colA{A}\lor \colC{C})" display />

<Math tex="\tag{4}\colA{A}\lor(\colA{A}\land \colB{B})=\colA{A},\quad \colA{A}\land(\colA{A}\lor \colB{B})=\colA{A}" display />

<Math tex="\tag{5}\colA{A}\lor\neg \colA{A}=1,\quad \colA{A}\land\neg \colA{A}=0" display />

Laws 1–5 are associativity, commutativity, distributivity, absorption and complement. Both dual forms are permitted. Equality means logical equivalence, not identical writing. Whole expressions may replace $A$, $B$, $C$.

<span id="from-boolesche-grundgesetze-recognizing-the-shape-of-a-law"></span>

### Recognizing the shape of a law

Distributing $A \land (B\lor C)$ keeps $A$ in both alternatives: with $B$ or with $C$. Absorption adds no cases: $A\land B$ already requires $A$. These readings explain the laws, but do not replace restricted-law derivations.

## Sheet 1.1 · identity and idempotence

<span id="from-neutralitaet-und-idempotenz-two-helper-identities-from-absorption"></span>

### Two helper identities from absorption

Absorption (4), then complement (5), prove 7a and 7b as helpers for idempotence (6). Proof order may differ from exercise numbering; circular assumptions are forbidden.

<Math tex="\colA{A}\overset{4}=\colA{A}\lor(\colA{A}\land\neg \colA{A})\overset{5}=\colA{A}\lor0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \colA{A}\lor0=\colA{A}\quad(H_1)" display />

<Math tex="\colA{A}\overset{4}=\colA{A}\land(\colA{A}\lor\neg \colA{A})\overset{5}=\colA{A}\land1\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \colA{A}\land1=\colA{A}\quad(H_2)" display />


<span id="from-neutralitaet-und-idempotenz-6a--repeating-or-changes-nothing"></span>

### 6a · Repeating OR changes nothing

<Math tex="\begin{aligned}\colA{A}\lor \colA{A}&\overset{H_2}=(\colA{A}\lor \colA{A})\land1\\&\overset{5}=(\colA{A}\lor \colA{A})\land(\colA{A}\lor\neg \colA{A})\\&\overset{3}=\colA{A}\lor(\colA{A}\land\neg \colA{A})\\&\overset{5}=\colA{A}\lor0\\&\overset{H_1}=\colA{A}\end{aligned}" display />


<span id="from-neutralitaet-und-idempotenz-6b--the-dual-calculation"></span>

### 6b · The dual calculation

<Math tex="\begin{aligned}\colA{A}\land \colA{A}&\overset{H_1}=(\colA{A}\land \colA{A})\lor0\\&\overset{5}=(\colA{A}\land \colA{A})\lor(\colA{A}\land\neg \colA{A})\\&\overset{3}=\colA{A}\land(\colA{A}\lor\neg \colA{A})\\&\overset{5}=\colA{A}\land1\\&\overset{H_2}=\colA{A}\end{aligned}" display />

Both chains reverse distributivity. Duality swaps $\land,\lor$ and $0,1$; the explicit second chain exposes every permitted step.


<span id="from-neutralitaet-und-idempotenz-how-the-proof-is-found"></span>

### How the proof is found

Expand $A\lor A$ with $\land1$ using $H_2$, then replace $1$ by $A\lor\neg A$. Reverse distributivity combines the common $A$; complement removes the extra part, and $H_1$ finishes. Expansion creates the useful law pattern without assuming idempotence.


<span id="from-neutralitaet-und-idempotenz-practice-with-the-starting-laws"></span>

### Practice with the starting laws

Name the permitted rules.

<Exercise question="Reduce $(B \land  A) \lor  A$ using only laws 2 and 4.">

Commute both outer disjunction and inner conjunction to get $A\lor(A\land B)$; absorption gives $A$. No idempotence is used.

</Exercise>

## Sheet 1.1 · absorbing elements and constants

<span id="from-nullgesetze-und-konstanten-7c-and-7d--the-absorbing-values"></span>

### 7c and 7d · The absorbing values

<Math tex="\colA{A}\lor1\overset5=\colA{A}\lor(\colA{A}\lor\neg \colA{A})\overset1=(\colA{A}\lor \colA{A})\lor\neg \colA{A}\overset6=\colA{A}\lor\neg \colA{A}\overset5=1" display />

<Math tex="\colA{A}\land0\overset5=\colA{A}\land(\colA{A}\land\neg \colA{A})\overset1=(\colA{A}\land \colA{A})\land\neg \colA{A}\overset6=\colA{A}\land\neg \colA{A}\overset5=0" display />

Previously proved idempotence (6) is now available.


<span id="from-nullgesetze-und-konstanten-8--negating-constants"></span>

### 8 · Negating constants

<Math tex="1\overset5=0\lor\neg0\overset2=\neg0\lor0\overset{7a}=\neg0" display />

<Math tex="0\overset5=1\land\neg1\overset2=\neg1\land1\overset{7b}=\neg1" display />

Thus $\neg0=1$, $\neg1=0$, by numbered laws alone, without truth tables or De Morgan.


<span id="from-nullgesetze-und-konstanten-neutral-and-absorbing-elements"></span>

### Neutral and absorbing elements

Neutral $0$ leaves $A\lor0$ dependent on $A$; absorbing $1$ fixes $A\lor1$. For conjunction the roles reverse. To prove $A\lor1=1$, complement exposes two copies of $A$, associativity groups them and already-proved idempotence removes one.
