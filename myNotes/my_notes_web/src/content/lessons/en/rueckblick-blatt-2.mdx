## Keep the conditions attached

- The shear formula requires $i\ne j$.
- Diagonal inverses require every $d_i\ne0$.
- For sign matrices, even parity has a construction; odd parity is excluded by determinant $-1$.
- Uniqueness is relative to the allowed factors. For $R/L$ words, test the identity first and include $a=b$ in the R case.
- Shear equivalence permits the specified row/column additions, not arbitrary scaling.
- Matrix cancellation uses inverses on the side of the shared factor.
