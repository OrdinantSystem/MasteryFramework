# Research evidence checked for this batch

Checked 4 November 2026. Primary author abstracts were read through web tools; these are paraphrases, not downloaded full papers. Scope: only the modest claims listed here may be added. The protocol remains our proposed workflow.

| Source | What the primary abstract supports | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Roediger & Karpicke 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16507066/), DOI 10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x | Two prose-learning experiments compared free-recall tests without feedback with repeated study. Restudy did better at five minutes; prior testing did better at two days and one week. | A predicted gain for this course, all forms of transfer, an optimal personal interval, or the complete attempt/feedback/workflow. |
| [Cepeda et al. 2008, author institution record](https://digitalcommons.usf.edu/psy_facpub/1766/), DOI 10.1111/j.1467-9280.2008.02209.x | Fact learning with varied gaps and final delays found that the best gap depended on how far away the final test was. | Our specific day labels, the rule “always increase after success”, an optimal universal schedule, or proof of a particular memory mechanism. |
| [Rohrer, Dedrick & Burgess 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24578089/), DOI 10.3758/s13423-014-0588-3 | Existing interleaving lesson's 140 grade-7 students, nine weeks of practice, two-week delayed unannounced test and means 72%/38% agree with primary abstract. | Forecast benefit for our authored proof tasks or arbitrary subject switching. No change to the lesson in this batch. |
| [Karpicke & Blunt 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21252317/), DOI 10.1126/science.1199327 | Existing mapping lesson's limited comparison agrees with the primary abstract: retrieval exceeded the studied concept-mapping condition on science-text comprehension/inference. | All mapping is ineffective, nor validation of this complete course. No strengthened claim planned. |
