# Learning & Teaching: first-batch retrospective

Completed locally on 4 November 2026. The result is a more accessible entry and more checkable practice in five lesson identities, in English and German. Twelve other identities remain unchanged. The evidence supports specific editorial, preservation and site-behavior conclusions; it does not demonstrate faster learning or better retention.

## Starting point and learner

The course already had complete editorial work across seventeen lessons, including Bloom, Feynman explanation, mapping, retrieval, spacing, interleaving, tutoring, evidence, records and four distinct critical transcript guides. The Elephant read every EN/DE lesson, including answers, and inspected the complete reusable learning components/data and template strings. Rewriting every page would have discarded useful work without an identified need.

The intended reader is a university learner who may be new to study methods and may not study mathematics. Some opening examples required linear maps or convergence. Existing Study Sessions and Proof Practice suggested parity tasks without complete local reference arguments. These were concrete first-batch gaps. The [audit](audit-and-brief.md) records retain/reorder/clarify/add dispositions for every lesson and explains deferred specialist examples.

## What changed and why

| Lesson | Change | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learning Methodology | Familiar goal and links to retrieval/general session instructions; local real-domain linearity/zero explanation | Reach a useful own-subject attempt without completing specialist examples first; preserve original reasoning |
| Bloom’s Taxonomy | Familiar task comparison and precise local sequence/bound/threshold vocabulary before existing lens | Make task categories understandable while retaining the mathematical comparison and nonranking caveat |
| Retrieval and Spaced Practice | Separate attempt, timing and mixing; link the existing worked correction; add a planning task and limited primary evidence | Make the next study decision inspectable without duplicating the mapping narrative or claiming subject transfer |
| Study Sessions | Definitions, full odd-square worked proof, visible changed application and disclosed complete references, plus a delayed variation | Let a reader check the existing practice while distinguishing reconstruction, exposure, hints and later performance |
| Proof Practice | Complete two-/three-odd references and explicit parity/proof request | Preserve the graduated hints and give the learner a complete answer for comparison |

The existing process graphs, Bloom lens, spacing timeline, mind-map interaction and shared design remain. A Markdown comparison table was sufficient for the independent mechanism axes. No component, preset, CSS, template, dependency, route identity/order, tutor backend or scoring scheme was added. Existing source caveats, meaningful tasks, old reference answers, headings/anchors and all metadata remain.

## Limits and next scope decision

No learner trial, delayed learning study, human native-language/mathematical certification, physical touch/pen test or screen-reader audit occurred. The two new research statements were checked against primary author abstracts, not full papers; they support only limited task/timing comparisons. The exact workflow and adaptive intervals remain proposed choices. Original private learning sources were unavailable in this checkout, so their historical quotations and identities were retained without new certification. They were not copied into public output.

Retain this batch as a source-checked, reviewed editorial improvement. Broader non-mathematical support for Feynman/matrix examples, another figure or a different course sequence should depend on a concrete issue or learner evidence. Do not expand automatically. A useful later learner check would ask for a study plan, its rationale, an actual unfamiliar subject attempt with assistance/exposure recorded, and a later changed attempt. Such evidence could motivate retain/revise/expand decisions; it is not supplied by these build or agent reviews.
