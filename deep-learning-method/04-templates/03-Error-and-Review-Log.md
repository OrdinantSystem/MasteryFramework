## Review rules

- Do not call an error repaired just because the correction made sense.
- Mark repaired only after successful independent retry on a different task.
- Mark durable only after a delayed independent task; record date.
- Treat planned dates as adjustable; prioritize persistent weaknesses and important prerequisites.
