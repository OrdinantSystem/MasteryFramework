# Reading and Evidence

## Directly relevant research

1. **Dunlosky et al. (2013)**, *Improving Students’ Learning With Effective Learning Techniques*, Psychological Science in the Public Interest. Evaluates practice testing and distributed practice, among other techniques. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266
2. **Rohrer & Taylor (2007)**, *The shuffling of mathematics problems improves learning*, Instructional Science. Research on mixed/interleaved mathematics practice. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s11251-007-9015-8
3. **Chi et al. (1989)**, *Self-explanations: How students study and use examples in learning to solve problems*, Cognitive Science. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1302_1
4. **Bastani et al. (2025)**, *Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning: Evidence from high school mathematics*, PNAS. Evidence that supported practice performance may diverge from independent learning: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12232635/
5. **Dunlosky & Rawson (2019)**, *Practice Tests, Spaced Practice, and Successive Relearning: Tips for Classroom Use and for Guiding Students’ Learning*, Scholarship of Teaching and Learning in Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1037/stl0000128

These sources support **components and relevant risks**. They do not establish that the precise DLM workflow, time allocations, or review intervals are globally optimal.

## MIT/NotebookLM story: provenance and caution

- Social-media origin discussed in research: https://x.com/ihtesham2005/status/2030214970353602806
- Written protocol inspired by story: https://github.com/Corvus400/notebooklm-study-kit
- Its method document: https://github.com/Corvus400/notebooklm-study-kit/blob/main/METHOD.md

The named student, outcomes (48 hours replacing five months; 90-minute compression), and any MIT endorsement were **not independently verified** in the earlier investigation. The design here is our own synthesized, testable study protocol, not a claim of MIT authority.

## Attribution / source-of-truth practice

For actual course work, trust official lecture notes/syllabus for the course's scope and notation. Distinguish: `SOURCE CLAIM` (with page/section), `AGENT SYNTHESIS`, `STUDENT PROOF`, `UNVERIFIED`. AI-generated citations require checking before they are accepted.
