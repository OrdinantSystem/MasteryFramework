# Change Log and Retrospective

## Version history

### v0.1.0 — 2026-11-02

**Baseline release.** Combines concept maps, guided derivation, scaffolded problem-solving, proof-oriented challenges, specific feedback, and spaced retention. Uses a hint ladder H0–H5 and six-dimensional mastery rubric. This is an experimental design based on prior tutoring experience and established component-level learning research, not a proven complete intervention.

## Monthly retrospective prompts

1. Which independent tasks still fail despite apparently clear explanations?
2. Are hints arriving too quickly or too late?
3. What type of mistake dominates: conceptual, logical, procedural, strategic, representation, communication, or prerequisite?
4. Does delayed performance match immediate performance?
5. Is the process sustainable alongside normal life and coursework?
6. What **one** adjustment deserves a controlled test next?

## Governance rule

Treat changes as hypotheses. Do not rewrite the entire system based on one excellent or disappointing exam. Keep earlier files or Git history if possible, and document why a change was made.
